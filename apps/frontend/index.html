About the Institute

Preparing graduates for professional and community impact.

IDMC provides competency-based education that connects classroom learning with practical institutional, business and community needs.

Our integrated information system supports applicants, students, lecturers and administrators throughout the complete academic lifecycle.

Integrity Responsible leadership and ethical service.
