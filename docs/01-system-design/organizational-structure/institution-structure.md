# IDMC ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

## Institution
Institute of Development and Medical Sciences (IDMC)

## Institution Type
Private Higher Learning Institution

## Main Organizational Hierarchy

Institution
    ↓
Campus
    ↓
School / Faculty
    ↓
Department
    ↓
Programme
    ↓
Curriculum
    ↓
Academic Year
    ↓
Semester
    ↓
Course

## Design Principles

- The system must support multiple campuses.
- A school/faculty can contain multiple departments.
- A department can contain multiple programmes.
- A programme can have multiple curriculum versions.
- A curriculum belongs to an academic year/version.
- A curriculum contains courses.
- Courses can be offered in different semesters.
- Students must be linked to a programme, campus and academic structure.
- Staff and lecturers must be linked to departments.
- The system must preserve historical academic records.
- Changing a programme or curriculum must not destroy previous student records.
